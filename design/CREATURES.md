# Hearthline Creatures

**Design status:** `PROPOSED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`

**Run status:** `PREPARED_NOT_RUN`

A **Creature** is a manifest-bound composition of separately identified
Hearthline Sparks assembled for one bounded task. It is the proposed body
around the bundle: membership, routing, evidence projection, budgets, return
paths, and effect boundaries become inspectable without confusing the bundle
with a model, a person, a new Spark role, or a source of authority.

For the affectionate Creature vocabulary, Light Trio formation checklist,
one-way Homecoming route, and bounded correspondence to CHARTER v1.0, see the
[`CHARTER Creature Field Guide`](CHARTER_CREATURE_FIELD_GUIDE.md). That
same-author narrative adaptation does not amend the station's frozen source
lock or authorize a run.

## Layering

```text
reasoning provider(s) -> separate Spark proposals and notes
                                  |
                     Creature manifest and controller
                       |          |          |
                  Work Spark   Scribes   Ledger Scribe
                       \          |          /
                        committed filtered Run Trail
                                  |
                 serialized admitted effect
                                  |
          separately authorized broker/domain writer
                                  |
        frozen terminal source -> projection-only adapter
```

Strongwiz may inspire or later support parts of this body, but the names are
not interchangeable. Strongwiz v3 does not currently implement a
multi-Creature coordinator, and this branch imports no Strongwiz code.

## Creature manifest

Before dispatch, one immutable manifest must bind:

- Creature ID and profile version;
- exact title branch, parent anchor, source lock, and design version;
- every member Spark, role, job, profile, provider artifact, grant, budget,
  active Static reference, isolated ledger, and Home;
- which Work Spark is paired with which one non-recursive Ledger Scribe;
- the committed event projection each member may receive;
- authenticated external operator-control, canonical controller, and
  separately authorized broker/domain-writer identities;
- external-effect, retry, reconciliation, suspension, and return rules;
- Thulia's partitioned Perch index and permitted Bridge-Gloss routes;
- frozen terminal-authority source, projection-only adapter, official
  environment, and evaluator identities; and
- residuals, exclusions, claim ceiling, and reopening conditions.

An unbound field remains `UNBOUND`; it is not filled from historical habit.
Changing any identity-bearing field requires a successor manifest rather than
an in-place rewrite.

## Member isolation

Every Spark retains its own:

- ordered Spark and profile identity;
- role and exact grant;
- provider identity and context;
- budget and consumption record;
- Static version and residual lane;
- Field Notes, Embers, and ledger;
- heartbeat contract; and
- Home and Homecoming series.

Shared infrastructure does not join those identities. A Ledger Scribe follows
only the committed, grant-filtered projection. It may propose a target-bound
representation delta in its own lineage, but it cannot inspect hidden
reasoning, select Work actions, approve its own carry, write Work Static, or
receive its own recursive Ledger Scribe.

## Thulia's boundary

Thulia is a non-governing Owl Scribe interface. Her partitioned index may bind
each member to its own Perch and she may reconstruct selected sending-Spark
Static into a numbered, source-bound Bridge Gloss for one named receiver and
purpose.

She does not:

- merge ledgers or create a Creature-wide codebook;
- inspect or decide the primary task;
- rank or reconcile Spark proposals;
- approve carry or activate Static;
- select or release an action;
- grant permission, budget, or authority; or
- become the Creature's canonical controller.

“Keeping the Creature in check” therefore means preserving partition,
identity, reconstruction, and return provenance. Governance and effects remain
control-owned.

## Effect discipline

Authenticated external operator control grants or revokes permission. The
canonical controller admits and serializes proposals. It does not execute an
external effect. A separately authorized broker/domain writer executes and
records the exact admitted effect. Parallel Sparks may inspect the same
committed snapshot through different declared views and may emit proposals or
notes. They do not call the environment in parallel.

Before any future effect, the controller must revalidate the exact observation,
proposal, route, grant, budget, environment, adapter, executor, and one-use
permit. A completed effect receives a distinct outcome receipt. If dispatch may
have occurred but its outcome is unknown, the Creature stops and reconciles;
uncertainty never authorizes retry. A frozen terminal-authority source
establishes terminal observation. The adapter validates and projects that
source but cannot manufacture task, result, or terminal status.

## Heartbeats and suspension

Each Spark is bound to a task-shaped heartbeat contract. Productive pulses require a
material change such as a checkpoint, gate, budget band, risk, residual,
steering aperture, or terminal-state change. An unchanged work projection may
have separately declared audit motion, but a timer tick alone creates no
progress claim.

A heartbeat:

- is status and receipt data only;
- cannot renew scope, authority, time, calls, or budget;
- cannot prove progress, completion, consciousness, or permission;
- cannot keep a Codex workspace alive or guarantee that a process or handoff
  will continue; and
- cannot wake a suspended Spark without a valid controller-owned resume
  receipt.

## Open objective windows

The controller or product may own an open objective window. A suspended Spark
or Creature does not monopolize that window: the owner may admit new objectives
while preserving the suspension honestly. Admission does not reopen, rewrite,
or enlarge the suspended objective.

Every admitted objective binds a separate objective ID, Spark or Creature,
scope, grant, budget, ledger, heartbeat contract, and Homecoming. Events carry
those references forward; an event bearing another objective's reference is a
scope error. Objective disposition and Homecoming custody state are independent
coordinates. Custody reconciliation cannot manufacture objective or result
status. Rule-established results remain in the owning evaluator's namespace;
window-level handoff values remain qualified, for example
`OBJECTIVE:BLOCKED`, `OBJECTIVE:CANCELLED`, or `OBJECTIVE:LEFT_OPEN`.

Completions may return out of intake order. The aggregate response closes only
against an explicit objective-set snapshot recording every expected
objective's separate Homecoming custody state and rule-owned or handoff
disposition. Cancelled, blocked, unknown, and deliberately open objectives stay
visible and do not become completion.

An interrupt heartbeat may bind a suspension or resume checkpoint. It does not
schedule the next objective, keep the workspace alive, enlarge a grant, or
release an effect. The controller only admits and serializes. Any external
effect requires an operator grant and a separately authorized broker/domain
writer, and each completed effect has one unique receipt.

The synthetic A/B/C fixture exercises only these static invariants: suspend A;
admit B and C; return C then B with synthetic rule-owned dispositions; resume
and return A; then close one aggregate response. Passing validation is not a
live concurrency, scheduling, or task-success result.

## Matched evaluation design

A later authorized evaluation must use at least two physically isolated arms:

1. an otherwise matched control without the Creature mechanism under test;
2. a Creature candidate with the declared bundle and representation path.

Each arm begins in its own absent-or-empty root with a distinct ledger. The
operator, official environment, game, seed, initial state, toolbelt,
presentation, and action/reset/call/time/context/compute/memory budgets must be
matched. An external campaign index may bind identities, plans, checkpoints,
and seals only. It must not become a shared payload ledger, learned-state lane,
or covert communication path.

The current synthetic fixture declares this topology but does not instantiate
physical labs or evaluate a mechanism.

## Current stop boundary

No current official ARC framework, package, game, evaluator, provider, or
budget is bound. `EvidenceYieldGate` is not wired into a runner, and no
multi-Creature coordinator exists. This branch therefore stops at design,
schema, fabricated fixture, and offline verification.
