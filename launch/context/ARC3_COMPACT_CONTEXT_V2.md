## Common shell

The environment is the world and referee. `FrameData` is the sensory heartbeat. `MyAgent` is a bounded controller/player-policy surface, not the environment and not a source of permission. One `GameAction` is proposed at a time; only the official runtime can establish its consequence.

Keep six kinds apart: direct observation, derived state, hypothesis, plan, action, and outcome. Distinction grouping may further organize them, but never promotes one group into another. `advance` is atomic: a frozen rule either installs one complete successor field with all evidence handles, or leaves the candidate in hypotheses with a residual. It never partly mutates the current model or edits a predecessor.
