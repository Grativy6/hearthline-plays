## Findings carried into the design

1. **Representation needs its own bounded job.** The scribe receives
   receipt-bound derived summaries, freezes a proposal before held-out
   evaluation, and has no action or authority port.
2. **Future telemetry must count every cost surface.** Source, residual,
   compact, request, response, codebook, evaluation, review, adoption,
   transfer, checkpoint, heartbeat, and capsule bytes are requirements for a
   future runner. The prototype has not established complete telemetry, and
   gross compression is not net savings.
3. **Restart is a transport claim.** A checkpoint restores only its declared
   work projection and must revalidate code, dependencies, environment,
   grants, budgets, residuals, trace head, and external-effect boundary.
4. **A pulse is not progress.** Only a fresh material cause emits a productive
   heartbeat. Administrative stutter remains an audit coordinate with no work
   motion or authority.
5. **Action roles stay separate.** Authenticated external operator control
   grants or revokes; the canonical controller admits and serializes; a
   separately authorized broker/domain writer executes and records. An
   uncertain effect is not silently retried.
6. **Comparisons require physical isolation.** Control and candidate arms use
   separate absent-or-empty roots and ledgers. An external campaign index may
   bind plans, identities, checkpoints, and seals but not shared payload state.
7. **Terminal authority stays external.** A complete-looking representation, score,
   level transition, confidence statement, or synthetic pass cannot replace
   the frozen terminal-authority source. An adapter may validate and project
   that source; it cannot establish the terminal observation itself.
