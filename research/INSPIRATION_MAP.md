independent corroboration, or that any proposed mechanism improves ARC
performance. Exact identities are in [`sources.lock.json`](sources.lock.json).

## Relationship vocabulary

- `DECLARED_MECHANICAL_CONTEXT` means a source supplies a named design
  vocabulary under its own ceiling.
- `RESEARCH_CONTEXT` means a result or fixture may inspire a bounded proposal.
- `DESIGN_INSPECTION` means exact source was reviewed without importing or
  executing it in this station.
- `ADAPTED_IMPLEMENTATION` would require a separately identified implementation,
  attribution, change record, and tests. No station source currently has this
  relationship.
- `EXECUTED_CODE` would require a run receipt binding the exact code and
  environment. No station source currently has this relationship.

The map is prospective. A future implementation must append its own evidence;
later success cannot backdate causal participation into this preparation pass.
