# Founding boundary

## What begins here

This subtree begins a bounded Hearthline/PAL mathematical playground under
the stewardship of Christopher D. Pang. It descends from the published
pre-Astra lineage anchor `PRE-ASTRA-20260904T001827Z` at Git commit
`ef18554b2ca828b270dfb78512550b9b401ab6e4`.

That earlier anchor is not rewritten. This genesis is a successor event.

The founding scope is deliberately narrow:

1. continue the Riemann line from an opaque, privately held checkpoint;
2. open a P-versus-NP finite-game laboratory;
3. resolve “geometry” into a solved Poincare control lane and a separately
   gated, open Hodge lane.

The remaining Clay problems are source-locked for orientation but are not
opened as active arenas by this genesis.

## Credit and authority

Christopher D. Pang is the human author, research steward, and accountable
editor. Hearthline and PAL are disclosed AI-assisted research tools. They are
not authors, people, governing authorities, proof authorities, or sources of
mathematical acceptance.

Artifact-level records control over any project-level shorthand. Human review
or adoption does not erase model assistance, and model assistance does not
create authorship or authority.

## Three statements that must never collapse

- The steward's conceptual satisfaction is real personal understanding; it is
  not a public proof claim.
- A sealed artifact has stable, checkable bytes and stated ancestry; sealing
  does not make its mathematics correct.
- A proof candidate may be serious and complete-looking; it remains a
  candidate until the exact target, dependencies, and gaps survive competent
  external review.

## No-Astra boundary

For this genesis construction, no output knowingly presented as Astra was
admitted. That statement is session-record-declared, not provider-attested. It
cannot exclude hidden provider routing, training ancestry, omitted outside
interactions, or a different system using the same name.

The private Riemann checkpoint's historical model ancestry is not inferred by
this public receipt. Its exact identity and reveal material remain outside
Git. Unknown ancestry stays unknown; it is never upgraded by inheritance.

## Publication and prize boundary

Clay does not accept direct proposed solutions. Under its revised rules, a
candidate must be a complete mathematical solution of the official Problem
description, be published in a qualifying outlet, wait at least two years,
and achieve general acceptance before it is eligible for CMI consideration.
CMI determines whether those conditions are met, whether detailed review is
merited, and whether a prize is awarded. This repository therefore never
self-assigns those external states.

See the locked rules source in
[`research/clay-official-sources.lock.json`](research/clay-official-sources.lock.json).
